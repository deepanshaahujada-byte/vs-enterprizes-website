Featured role

Procurement & Materials Manager (Construction Projects)

HQ-based, multi-site coordination Full-time Construction / Infrastructure / EPC CTC: ₹4.2-7.2 LPA Reports to Partner / Management

Centralise and streamline procurement across multiple construction projects by owning end-to-end material planning, vendor management, commercial negotiations and purchase execution, ensuring cost efficiency, timely material availability and minimal site disruption.

Material planning & demand management
  • Collect, consolidate and validate material requirements from multiple project sites weekly
  • Align material demand with BOQs, project timelines and execution stages
  • Prevent overstocking, shortages and emergency procurement through proactive planning
Vendor sourcing & management
  • Identify, onboard and manage vendors across material categories: cement, steel, electrical, plumbing, finishing and more
  • Obtain and compare quotations, minimum 2-3 vendors for major items
  • Negotiate pricing, credit terms, delivery schedules and commercial conditions
  • Maintain an approved vendor master and rate database
Purchase orders & procurement execution
  • Prepare, issue and track purchase orders per the approval matrix
  • Ensure accuracy in specifications, quantities, GST compliance, freight terms and payment conditions
  • Coordinate vendor dispatch schedules and delivery confirmations
Site & logistics coordination
  • Act as the single point of contact between site teams and vendors
  • Ensure timely material delivery to avoid work stoppages or idle manpower
  • Resolve supply issues, delays and vendor disputes in real time
Who we are looking for
  • 3-6 years in procurement or materials management within construction or EPC projects; multi-site exposure preferred
  • Strong negotiation and commercial acumen, with working knowledge of construction materials, specifications and market pricing
  • Proficient in MS Excel or Google Sheets (mandatory); familiar with GST invoices, transport logistics and basic compliance
  • Process-driven, detail-oriented, assertive and solution-focused under pressure
