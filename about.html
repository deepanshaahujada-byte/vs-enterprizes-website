VS Enterprises is a women-owned, MSME-registered partnership firm (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise), incorporated on 1 January 2022. We build and deliver civil infrastructure and renewable energy projects for India's public sector.

Headquartered in Lucknow with a corporate office in Noida, we work across Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Punjab.

We are a principal EPC contractor (Engineering, Procurement & Construction): we bid for our own work and run it ourselves, from tender to handover.

In under 4 years, VS Enterprises has become a trusted partner to India's premier defense and paramilitary organisations, including the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and the Border Security Force (BSF). We keep winning repeat work because we show up, stay compliant, and finish the job.

Vision

To grow into a nationally scalable infrastructure and renewable energy platform, aligned with India's development priorities.

Mission

  • Technically robust delivery — quality-driven infrastructure, built to last.
  • Regulatory discipline — strict adherence to public procurement rules on every bid.
  • Multi-location scale — systems built for simultaneous execution across states.
  • Renewable growth — solar and manufacturing capability as its own expanding line of business.
