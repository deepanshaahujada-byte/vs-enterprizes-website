Who we are

A women-owned MSME. A principal EPC contractor. A partner India's institutions trust with their toughest sites.

VS Enterprises is a women-owned, MSME-registered partnership firm, incorporated on 1 January 2022, engaged in the execution of civil infrastructure and renewable energy projects across India's public-sector ecosystem.

Headquartered in Lucknow with a corporate office in Noida, the firm operates across Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Punjab, delivering multi-site institutional projects with structured governance and execution discipline.

We function as a principal EPC contractor, managing projects end to end, from tender participation and contract acquisition to on-ground execution, compliance management and project handover.

“The management expertise required for construction supervision is one of the scarcest resources of the industry all over the world.”
Patricia M. Hillebrandt, construction economist

In under two years, VS Enterprises has become a trusted partner to India's premier defense and paramilitary organisations, including the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and the Border Security Force (BSF). Our ability to secure repeat work orders reflects structured tender participation, strong institutional alignment and disciplined compliance management.

Vision

To emerge as a nationally scalable infrastructure and renewable energy platform delivering civil works, sustainable energy solutions, and institutional supply capabilities aligned with India's development priorities.

Mission

Deliver technically robust and quality-driven infrastructure projects. Maintain strict adherence to public procurement and regulatory frameworks. Develop scalable systems for multi-location execution. Integrate renewable energy and manufacturing within the institutional infrastructure value chain.
