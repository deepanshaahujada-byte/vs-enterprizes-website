Public procurement has changed. Have your bids?

From the Central Public Procurement Portal and Government e-Marketplace to GFR 2017, procurement manuals and EPC contracting, the Government of India has rewritten how goods, services and works are bought. Preferential access for MSMEs and domestic manufacturers has raised the stakes further.

VS Enterprises delivers tailor-made solutions that bring efficiency to the way projects are procured and executed, backed by a network of experienced professionals across the public-procurement domain.
