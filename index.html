A trusted partner to India's institutional ecosystem

VS Enterprises is a women-owned, MSME-registered partnership firm incorporated on 1 January 2022, engaged in the execution of civil infrastructure and renewable energy projects across India's public sector. We function as a principal EPC contractor, managing projects end to end, from tender participation and contract acquisition to on-ground execution, compliance management and handover.

Headquartered in Lucknow with a corporate office in Noida, we operate across Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Punjab, delivering multi-site institutional projects with structured governance and execution discipline.

What we do

Three core areas of expertise, one principal EPC contractor.

Institutional & Civil Infrastructure

Execution of structural and campus-based infrastructure, from multi-storey barracks to administrative complexes, for defense and paramilitary organisations.

Institutional Housing

Construction of Type IV, Type V and other categorised residential quarters for institutional campuses.

Mess & Administrative Buildings

Functional support infrastructure for large-scale campus operations.

Rehabilitation & Prefab Works

Structural rehabilitation, modernisation and rapid-deployment prefabricated structures.

RCC Approach Roads

High-durability reinforced cement concrete road networks built for heavy institutional traffic and long-term resilience.

Renewable Energy Infrastructure

144 solar water heating sites executed for the Border Security Force, with multi-brand, multi-location deployment capability to BIS and MNRE specification.

Infrastructure & Utilities Coordination

End-to-end management of site infrastructure, drainage systems and essential utility networks ensuring operational readiness.
