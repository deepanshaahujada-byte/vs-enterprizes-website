What we offer

EPC execution on-site, backed by fast, responsive, strategic advice from experienced subject-matter experts.

Strategy rooted in your values

As an EPC contractor, we carry projects from tender to on-site delivery, so our strategic advice is grounded in execution reality, not theory. Our recommended strategies are always aligned with our clients' values, vision and mission, rooted in a holistic viewpoint that captures every aspect of a project and the interconnections between them.

VS experts have the experience and know-how to identify issues, analyse their potential impact, propose solutions, and monitor their execution in an efficient and cost-effective manner. We specialise in managing, monitoring and controlling major developments.

We also resolve disputes during the pendency of projects, with a robust dispute-resolution mechanism that keeps work moving.

How we do it

VS brings value by transforming project management. Our strength is a nuanced understanding of procurement and contracting, applied across the end-to-end process.

01

Identifying opportunity

With our vast network of public-procurement professionals across our focus sectors, we coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to follow emerging trends and generate high-quality leads that convert into relevant opportunities.

02

Facilitating strategic bidding

Over 20 years of mentor experience and 1000+ tender applications inform how we guide clients through the bidding process:

  • Drafting and reviewing all relevant and crucial tender documentation, so clients arrive prepared
  • Insightful recommendations that fast-track the process and track complex, ever-changing requirements, keeping every application compliant and clear of rejection
  • A set of best practices curated from rigorous analysis of past tender performance and professional knowledge
03

Correspondence and monitoring

Our networking executives maintain constant follow-ups with the buyer throughout the process:

  • Meetings with engineering teams to understand buyer requirements and customise the client's solution
  • Maintaining a favourable relationship with the buyer and responding to every query
  • Proactive suggestions that streamline the buyer's process in the client's interest
  • Monitoring every project's status, pursuing updates, and reporting progress to the client
